LOGISTICS WORK DESK - GOI DUNG THU (du lieu mau, khong phai du lieu that)
===========================================================

Goi nay da co san phan giao dien da dong goi. Khong can GitHub, khong can
go lenh. Chi can bam chuot.

-----------------------------------------------------------
CACH MO (lam 1 lan moi khi dung)
-----------------------------------------------------------
1. Giai nen goi ZIP nay ra mot thu muc MOI (vi du: Desktop).
   - Dung giai nen vao thu muc dang chua ung dung that cua cong ty.

2. Neu dung MAY MAC:
   Nhap dup vao tep:  MO-APP-DUNG-THU-MAC.command
   - Neu macOS hien canh bao: nhap CHUOT PHAI vao tep -> chon "Open"
     -> bam "Open" lan nua.
   - Neu nhap dup ma tep mo ra bang trinh soan thao (thay vi chay):
     nhap CHUOT PHAI vao tep -> "Open With" -> chon "Terminal".
   - Lan chay dau tien mat 1-3 phut de tu cai thu vien (can Internet).
   - Trinh duyet se tu mo ung dung. Neu chua mo, bam vao:
     http://localhost:9501

3. Neu dung WINDOWS:
   Nhap dup vao tep:  MO-APP-DUNG-THU-WINDOWS.cmd
   (May can co Python 3.11 tro len. Neu chua co, Windows se bao va mo trang
   tai Python mien phi.)

-----------------------------------------------------------
TAT UNG DUNG
-----------------------------------------------------------
Dong cua so den vua hien ra (hoac bam Control + C trong cua so do).
Du lieu mau trong bo nho se tu bien mat - khong con gi tren may.

-----------------------------------------------------------

   ban do bien Viet Nam (co chu VIET NAM, BIEN DONG, Hoang Sa, Truong Sa).
2. Bam vao mot node tren ban do (vi du ON BOARD) -> bong bong thong tin va
   form cong viec mo ra ben phai.
3. Mo lo SEA-188 -> phai bao dung "chua co so do Journey", khong bia cong doan.
4. Trong form ON BOARD: go ngay thu 2026-10-04 -> bam "Luu moc co bang chung"
   -> tai lai trang (F5) -> ngay do van con. (Day chi la du lieu thu.)
5. Tren dien thoai: dung ngon tay keo ban do de di chuyen.
